What is phishing?

Phishing is a trick where someone pretends to be a bank, a company or a person you trust, so that you give them your password, OTP, card details or money.

Where phishing comes from

Email

"Your account is suspended. Click here to verify." Often with a logo copied from the real company.

SMS (smishing)

"Dear customer, your KYC is pending. Update now or your account will be blocked today."

WhatsApp

Fake job offers, lottery prizes, "electricity bill unpaid" messages, or a friend's hacked account asking for money.

QR codes

A QR code to "receive" money. Scanning a QR code is only ever needed to pay, never to receive.

How to spot a fake link

  1. Read the real domain. In paypal.com.secure-login.tk/ the real website is secure-login.tk, not PayPal. The real domain is the part just before the first single /.
  2. Look for spelling tricks. amaz0n.in, hdfcbank-update.com, rnicrosoft.com (r + n looks like m).
  3. Be careful with short links like bit.ly or tinyurl. They hide where they really go.
  4. The padlock is not enough. Phishing sites can also have HTTPS. It only means the connection is encrypted, not that the site is honest.
  5. Notice the pressure. "Within 24 hours", "last warning", "your account will be blocked" are made to stop you from thinking.

Golden rules

  • No bank, government office or company will ever ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password.
  • Open important websites by typing the address yourself or using the official app.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication on email, banking and social media.
  • If a friend asks for money on chat, call them first to confirm.
  • Use different passwords for different sites, or a password manager.

If you already clicked

  • Close the page. Do not type anything more.
  • Change the password of that account, and any account using the same password.
  • If you shared card or bank details, call your bank immediately to block them.
  • In India, report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 as soon as possible. Quick reporting can help stop the money transfer.
  • Report the link to PhishGuard so others are warned.
