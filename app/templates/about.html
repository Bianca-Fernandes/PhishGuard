About PhishGuard

PhishGuard (Phishing Attacks Detection System) is a BCA Vth semester major project at the School of Computer Applications, JECRC University, Jaipur.

How a scan works

  1. The URL is cleaned and only http / https links are accepted.
  2. Signals are collected from four checks: URL structure, a machine learning model, threat lists and domain details (RDAP, DNS, TLS).
  3. The risk engine combines the signals into a score from 0 to 100 and one of five risk levels.
  4. The result, the reasons and safety tips are shown and the scan is saved in history.

Risk levels

Very Low0 – 19
Low20 – 39
Medium40 – 59
High60 – 79
Critical80 – 100

Limitations

No phishing detector is perfect. A real website can sometimes look risky, and a brand-new phishing site can be missed. Outside services may be unavailable at times, and then PhishGuard uses the information it has. That is why the result is always shown as a risk, never as a guarantee.

Technology

HTML, CSS and JavaScript · Python and Flask · MySQL · scikit-learn · RDAP, DNS and TLS lookups · OpenPhish and Google Safe Browsing.
